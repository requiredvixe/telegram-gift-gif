## Telegram assets and data

This project renders public collectible-gift data and Lottie animations served by Telegram and Fragment. Telegram names, product marks, and gift artwork belong to their respective owners. This project is independent and is not endorsed by Telegram.

The bundled ribbon alpha mask was recreated from the publicly available Telegram iOS gift-ribbon geometry and is used only to reproduce the on-screen composition.

## SF Mono

SF Mono is an Apple font and is **not redistributed** in this repository. The renderer uses a legal system fallback by default. Users who already have a licensed copy may set `NUMBER_FONT_PATH` to reproduce the closest Telegram iOS number typography.
